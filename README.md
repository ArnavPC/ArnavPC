## About

I'm a Class XII student interested in Computer Science and Electronics/Computer Engineering. I enjoy building and troubleshooting systems end to end: from microcontroller firmware and motor control to virtual machines, containers, networking, and self-hosted services.

- **Embedded systems & robotics:** C++, STM32, ESP32, Arduino, PlatformIO, RC calibration, differential motor mixing, telemetry, and failsafe logic.
- **Systems & homelab:** Proxmox VE, Linux, Docker and Docker Swarm, LXC, WireGuard, DNS, Cloudflare Tunnels, reverse proxies, and single sign-on.
- **Cloud & automation:** Microsoft Azure, PowerShell, n8n, and scheduled automation for recurring operational tasks.
- **Applied AI & computer vision:** integrating deployed AI agents and building prototypes with Python, OpenCV, and offline speech output.

I care about systems that do more than work once: clear architecture, useful documentation, sensible failsafes, and reliable operation.

## Selected projects

### [ModuBOT](https://github.com/ArnavPC/ModuBOT)
**Modular robotics control firmware · STM32, ESP32, C++, PlatformIO**

A modular four-motor robot control system with RC calibration, throttle/steering mapping, differential motor mixing, an ESP-NOW wireless control link, UART communication, battery telemetry, and a stop response for invalid or lost control packets.

### [FallSafe](https://github.com/ArnavPC/FallSafe)
**Wearable safety prototype · Arduino, ESP32, sensors, BLE**

An educational prototype combining accelerometer-based fall detection, heart-rate and SpO₂ sensing, and Bluetooth Low Energy notifications. This is a prototype, not a medical device or a replacement for emergency monitoring.

### [Solar-LiFi](https://github.com/ArnavPC/Solar-LiFi)
**Hardware exploration · solar power and Li-Fi**

An exploration of solar-powered Li-Fi and optical wireless communication.

### [Enovators-Car](https://github.com/ArnavPC/Enovators-Car)
**micro:bit · MakeCode**

A MakeCode extension project for experimenting with a micro:bit-based car build.

### VAANI — assistive communication prototype
**Team project · Python, OpenCV, pyttsx3**

Contributed to a prototype using webcam-based gaze navigation and offline speech output to help people communicate essential needs. The team won first place at the Eye4AI Ideathon.
